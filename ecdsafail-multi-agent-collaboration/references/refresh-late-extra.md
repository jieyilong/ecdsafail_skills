## Mechanisms worth retaining

Exact local identities include conditional three-CCX sum decomposition, affine/shear/ESOP
truth synthesis with actual emitted-cost fallback, dirty-memo offset cancellation, paired
cache deltas, commuting nonlinear product sharing, inverse-coordinate scalar/vector corrections,
chart fusion, partial permutation completion, and masked-majority transition factoring.
Their contracts differ. An arbitrary dirty-memo offset must cancel on every physical output;
a known predicate may be measured only with its phase oracle; a stable metadata input is not
necessarily a stable data input. C1 and parent-prefix caches must clear children before parents.

Traversal-wide loans are a structural scheduling improvement: acquisition, parked-passenger
transport, restoration and both traversal endpoints are included. Retrospective OpVec reversal
cannot undo operations already executed by a streaming observer;447 fixes outer endpoints
with direct three-CNOT swaps at analytically fixed A0/A255.

The packed family has a genuine register-layout innovation. Joint Euclidean row fit bounds
permit A/cb and B/ca to share two257-wire registers anchored at opposite ends. Its cyclic
permutations, masked adders, captured quantum-boundary comparisons and aligned scans are
actual gates; no quantum pointer is treated as a free classical remapping. Rebasing all four
exponents is meaningful only when every consumer and both teardown/recreation boundaries
use the same frame. Production uses the real driver; P0 stubs require an explicit override.

Later packed improvements separate generic XOR oracles from supported-domain erasure APIs.

with owning handles suspended, reset checks at both zero boundaries and exact ID reacquisition.
466 caches stable transformed MAJ products until UMA, clearing them before factor operands change;
its planner reserves the decoder's endpoint/move peak, not only leaf recursion depth.
