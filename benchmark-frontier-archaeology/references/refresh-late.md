## Architectural transition

The [Skywalk submission 4f0d2135](https://github.com/Layr-Labs/ecdsafail-challenge/commit/8ee337cb69f7f56d4f04b03a951bf538479f2ee0) has official Q1174/T946250, score1110897500. It replaces the alternating signed odd-rail PingPong trajectory with a parity-routed signed binary-GCD realization of Stein's recurrence. In the raw sum/difference frame the rails begin at `(d+p,d)`, parity chooses the even rail, halving relabels wires, and a sign-controlled addition/subtraction updates the other rail. The orientation/sign pair has only three admitted symbols, so five steps occupy eight wires rather than ten. Fewer steps can outweigh the larger per-step routing and symbol cost.

Follow the active dispatcher: the new `heo.rs` header says the module is inert, but `mod.rs::build` installs `HEO_WALK=1`, `HEO_SCHEDULE=carry` and the complete baked recipe. `enabled()` routes division/multiplication into `heo_carry`. Treat the header as stale. The production carry schedule, rather than the raw reference walk, determines the measured width. It uses the FD handoff, partial P3/P5 tape decoding, head/fused/batch replay, terminal rail loans and direction-specific reconstruction. Payload cells call inherited finite-window PingPong helpers in the butterfly frame; they do not establish exact full-width modular arithmetic.

`Tape::pack5` can defer a measured phase bit until `unpack5` recreates its Boolean value. The division/multiplication orchestration consumes raw tape and deferred phase obligations before restoring the denominator interface. An allocator release needs its mathematical zero/sign-copy premise; `all_consumed` establishes bookkeeping consumption, not universal correctness of every precision envelope.

## Typed proof obligations

Keep four evidence classes distinct: an algebraic/Boolean identity under stated support; its state/frame and lifetime premises at the active emitter; source/operation binding; and the finite trusted benchmark packet. An MBU census reporting zero violations, a million-shot span screen, or a clean nonce does not become an all-input proof. A local SAT result supports only the exact modeled state and prefix assumptions; imported proof claims require their source anchor and transcript.

Replay layout affects precision as well as cost: a lower cap can create extra hot boundary compares or move a formerly exact leading chunk. A table row should carry cell index, direction, tick, bridge choice and room delta, with unique-index and range assertions. Raw expected cost, static CCX, sampled cohort cost and official rounded executed Toffoli are separate quantities.
